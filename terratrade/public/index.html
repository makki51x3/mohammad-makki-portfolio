      01 · About TerraTrade

      Local excellence, global demand.

      TerraTrade is an agricultural trading company based in Nigeria, committed to supplying high-quality agricultural products to global markets. Established in 2023, we specialise in sourcing, processing and packaging a diverse range of agricultural products, strictly adhering to international quality standards. Through strong partnerships with farmers and millers, we procure only the finest products, bridging the gap between producers and end-buyers.
